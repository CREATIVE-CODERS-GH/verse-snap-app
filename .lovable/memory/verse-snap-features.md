---
name: Verse Snap Bible feature boundaries
description: AO Lab lookup, guest library behavior, onboarding, and deferred feature constraints.
type: feature
---
Use AO Lab through a dedicated Bible service with only verified open translations. Reading, guest history, and guest saving do not require accounts. Keep signed-in cloud data private. Voice lookup (Web Speech) and camera scanning (on-device tesseract.js OCR at /scan) are implemented; Natural-language search is implemented: a model proposes candidate references only, and every verse shown is fetched and verified from AO Lab; no generated scripture, no chatbot UI.

Show a maximum-three-screen onboarding only on first use, remember completion locally, and always allow Skip. Keep the core terms exactly: Say a Verse, Snap a Verse, Search the Bible, Saved, History, Settings.
